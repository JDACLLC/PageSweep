Welcome to PageSweep

Capture the whole page.

Select the PageSweep toolbar icon once. Keep the tab visible while PageSweep scrolls, captures, and prepares one PNG.

Before capturing

Pin PageSweep from Chrome's extensions menu, then avoid switching tabs or interacting with the page until the capture finishes.

Where files go

PageSweep uses Chrome's normal download system. Files go to Chrome's configured download location—usually your Downloads folder.

If Chrome is set to ask where to save each file, Chrome will show that prompt.

Current limitations

  • Chrome-controlled pages such as chrome:// pages cannot be captured.
  • Exceptionally tall captures are scaled down to fit Chrome's image limits, so text may become difficult to read. Shorter captures work best until multipart PNG support is available.
  • Infinite feeds use safety limits and may not include every item.
  • PageSweep allows some time for delayed content to load, but may not capture every older message or embedded scrolling area.

Your privacy

PageSweep processes screenshots locally in Chrome and does not send captured webpage content to an external server.
